Wetlands & Waste Management Community Survey at Sebokeng

Rand Water: Drop by Drop Campaign

Thank you for participating in this survey. Your responses will help improve waste management and protect wetlands in our community.

1 Is household refuse (garbage) collected regularly in your area?
2 If garbage is not collected, where do households usually dispose of their waste?
3 Are there any official or safe dumping areas or refuse bins available near your home?
4 Do you think dumping waste in wetlands or near rivers affects water quality and community health?
5 What problems have you noticed because of dumping near wetlands or water areas? (You may choose more than one)
6 Would you be willing to participate in community activities to protect wetlands and save water?
7 What support would help your community reduce dumping and protect wetlands?
